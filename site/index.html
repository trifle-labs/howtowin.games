about this site

howtowin.games catalogs games alongside the best available answer to the question "how should I play this well?" — solution status, consensus strategy, and engine analysis. The search runs entirely in your browser: query vectors are computed with bge-small-en-v1.5 via transformers.js, and compared against pre-computed chunk vectors (meta + vectors, generated in CI).
