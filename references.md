# References

Master bibliography for `solved`. Game and lexicon entries link here by anchor
(e.g. `references.md#schaeffer2007`). Entries are ordered alphabetically by
anchor key.

Where the archive author has not personally verified a primary source, the
entry is marked **[verify]** — these citations should be checked against the
original before being relied upon.

---
### allis1988
Victor Allis. *A Knowledge-based Approach of Connect-Four — The Game is Solved:
White Wins.* M.Sc. thesis, Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, 1988. — First
published solution of standard 7×6 Connect Four; first player wins.

Link: <https://www.connectfour.net/Files/connect4.pdf> ([archive](http://web.archive.org/web/20260424013528/http://www.connectfour.net/Files/connect4.pdf))

### allis1994
Victor Allis. *Searching for Solutions in Games and Artificial Intelligence.*
Ph.D. thesis, University of Limburg, Maastricht, 1994. — Defines the
ultra-weakly / weakly / strongly solved hierarchy used throughout this archive;
also covers Gomoku, Qubic, Connect Four, Nine Men's Morris.

Link: <https://project.dke.maastrichtuniversity.nl/games/files/phd/allis_thesis.pdf>

### allis-gomoku1996
L. Victor Allis, H. Jaap van den Herik, M. P. H. Huntjens. *Go-Moku Solved by
New Search Techniques.* Computational Intelligence, 12(1):7–23, 1996. (Workshop
version 1993.) — Free-style Gomoku on 15×15 is a first-player win.

Link: <https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1467-8640.1996.tb00250.x> ([archive](http://web.archive.org/web/20231101131051/https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1467-8640.1996.tb00250.x))

### allis-pns1994
L. Victor Allis, Maarten van der Meulen, H. Jaap van den Herik. *Proof-Number
Search.* Artificial Intelligence, 66(1):91–124, 1994. — The PN-search technique
used to solve many games in this archive.

Link: <https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0004370294900043> ([archive](http://web.archive.org/web/20180821110815/https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0004370294900043))

### applegate-sprouts1991
David Applegate, Guy Jacobson, Daniel Sleator. *Computer Analysis of Sprouts.*
Technical report CMU-CS-91-144, Carnegie Mellon University, 1991. — Sprouts
analysed by computer up to 11 spots at the time.

Link: <https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~sleator/papers/Sprouts.htm> ([archive](http://web.archive.org/web/20260226123956/https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~sleator/papers/Sprouts.htm))

### ascher-mutorere1987

Link: <https://www.jstor.org/stable/2690367> ([archive](http://web.archive.org/web/20220202044955/https://www.jstor.org/stable/2690367))

### berlekamp-dotsandboxes2000

### bcg2001

Link: <https://www.routledge.com/Winning-Ways-for-Your-Mathematical-Plays-Volume-1/Berlekamp-Conway-Guy/p/book/9781568811307> ([archive](http://web.archive.org/web/20260217011838/https://www.routledge.com/Winning-Ways-for-Your-Mathematical-Plays-Volume-1/Berlekamp-Conway-Guy/p/book/9781568811307))

### gasser1996

### grundy1939
